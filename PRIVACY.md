# Privacy Policy for Guard Bot

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Guard** ("we", "our", or "the Bot") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Guild Data:** Server ID, channel IDs, and role settings.
- **User Data:** User IDs and moderation history (audit logs, bans, kicks).
- **Command Usage:** Interaction logs to ensure proper execution of security commands.

## 2. How We Use Information
All collected data is used exclusively for providing core bot functionality, such as automated threat detection, server protection, and log management.

## 3. Data Security & Storage
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide our security services.
